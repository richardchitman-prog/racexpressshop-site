How to use the Receipt Rewards Hub

This Hub is your own personal notebook for receipts — it doesn't scan anything or talk to the reward apps for you. Here's what to type into each box.

Store Type the name of where you shopped, like "Target" or "Kroger." Just a plain name — no need for an address or receipt number.
Amount ($) The total you spent on that receipt, before any rewards. If your receipt says $42.17, type 42.17.
Earned ($) What you expect to get back in cash/points value. This fills in on its own once you check off the apps below — you only need to change it if you already know the exact real amount an app gave you.
Date The day you actually shopped. If you skip this, it defaults to today's date.
Submitted to Tap every app you actually uploaded this receipt to. You can tap more than one — most people submit the same receipt to several apps at once. Tapping a bubble again un-selects it.
Log receipt Click this last, once everything above looks right. It saves the entry and adds it to your Ledger at the bottom of the page.
Quick walkthrough: Shop as usual and keep your receipt. Open each rewards app you use and submit the receipt there (that step still has to happen in each app — this Hub can't do it for you). Then come back here, fill in the four boxes, tap the apps you sent it to, and hit "Log receipt." Your running totals at the top of the page — Receipts logged, This month, Est. earned, and Top app — update automatically every time you do this. Made a mistake? Click the red ✕ next to any row in the Ledger to remove it.
